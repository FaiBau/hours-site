Terms of use

Hours is free. Members help each other and record the time in hours. FaiBau UG (haftungsbeschränkt) runs the app but is not part of the help members give each other.

§1 Who these terms apply to

These terms apply between FaiBau UG (haftungsbeschränkt) ("we") and everyone who creates an account in the Hours app ("members"). The community guidelines in the app are part of these terms.

§2 Membership

  1. You must be at least 18 years old.
  2. One account per person. Your details must be true; your username and photo must not pretend to be someone else.
  3. Everyone starts as a Newcomer. Members become Hourer and Trusted through vouches from other members and good references. Newcomers' own listings are checked before they go live, and Newcomers have lower hour limits.
  4. Keep your password to yourself. Tell us at once if someone else uses your account.

§3 How help works

  1. Members post offers ("I offer to...") and, once they have given hours, requests ("I need help with...").
  2. When a member accepts a listing, an HourDeal starts. Both agree on hours, time and place. The agreement is made between the two members only.
  3. After the help, both confirm the hours. One hour of help is one hour, whatever the skill. An HourDeal is 1, 2 or 3 hours.
  4. If only one side confirms, the HourDeal completes automatically after 7 days; the other side is reminded before that.
  5. If the two confirm different hours, Hours moderators decide.

§4 Hours are not money

  1. Hours record time given and received. They have no money value, cannot be bought, sold, paid out or exchanged for money or goods, and cannot be moved outside the app.
  2. Hours give no claim against us or against other members. A member with a negative balance owes no money.
  3. Balances have limits (currently -5 h to +5 h; for Newcomers -2 h to +2 h). We may change the limits for everyone.
  4. Costs for materials, machines or travel are settled directly between the members; we are not involved.
  5. Hours lapse when an account is deleted or ended.

§5 What is not allowed

  • Asking for or offering money, dating or sexual contact.
  • Help that needs a licence or professional qualification you don't have, or that is risky without one (for example medical, legal or tax advice, electrical work).
  • Care of people in need of care (nursing).
  • Anything illegal, discriminating, harassing or dangerous.
  • Advertising, spam, or using Hours to recruit for a business.
  • Fake references, several accounts, or getting around a ban.

Hours is not a dating service, not counselling or therapy, and not care or nursing.

§6 Your content

You are responsible for what you post. You allow us to show it in the app to other members for as long as it is online. You keep all rights to it.

§7 Moderation

To keep Hours safe we may check, send back or take down listings, remove references, cancel HourDeals, limit accounts and ban members who break these terms or the guidelines. We give a reason in the app. You can reply by email.

§8 Between members

  1. You decide whom you help and whom you meet. Meet in public places first. Never send money.
  2. HourDeals are not employment and not a service by us. We do not check members' skills.
  3. We recommend private liability insurance (Privathaftpflicht) that covers help for others.

§9 Liability

  1. We are liable without limit for intent and gross negligence, for injury to life, body or health, and under the Product Liability Act.
  2. For slight negligence we are liable only for breach of essential duties, limited to typical, foreseeable damage.
  3. We are not liable for what members do or fail to do in an HourDeal, or for the content they post.

§10 Availability

Hours is a free community project. We try to keep the app running but cannot promise it is always available. We may change, extend or stop features.

§11 Ending membership

You can delete your account in the app at any time (Me → Delete account). We can end a membership with two weeks' notice, or at once for a serious breach of these terms.

§12 Changes to these terms

We tell members about changes in the app at least four weeks before they take effect. If you don't agree, you can delete your account before then.

§13 Law

German law applies. If you are a consumer living in another EU country, its mandatory consumer protection rules still apply.
