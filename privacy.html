4. How long we keep data

  • As long as you have an account.
  • When you delete your account (Me → Delete account): your sign-in, email, profile details, photo, private details, push tokens, Inbox, chat messages you sent and the text of your references and vouches are erased. Open HourDeals are cancelled.
  • What stays: a nameless profile entry, the hours ledger and past HourDeals, so the other members' hour history stays correct. Nobody can see who you were.
  • Server logs: deleted automatically after a few days.
  • Where tax or commercial law requires longer storage, we keep that data only for that purpose.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15), correction (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). In the app: Me → Download my data gives you a file with your data; Me → Delete account erases your account. For anything else, email Fai@FaiBau.de.

You can complain to a data protection authority, for example the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.

6. Permissions on your phone

  • Photos: only when you choose a profile photo.
  • Notifications: only if you allow them; you can turn them off in your phone settings.

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

7. Minimum age

Hours is for people aged 18 and over.

8. Changes

We update this policy when Hours changes and tell members in the app about important changes. Last updated: 25 November 2026.
