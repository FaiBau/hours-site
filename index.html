The skill offering community

A community where we offer our skills to each other in hours. One hour of help is worth one hour, whoever you are.

The app is coming

How it works

  1. Offer an hourPick an idea or write your own: walking, languages, music, repairs, gardening and much more.
  2. Accept an offerDiscover what others offer.
  3. Agree on an HourDealFind someone in Berlin or online, choose 1, 2 or 3 hours, a time and a place, and chat in the app.
  4. Meet and confirmWhen you have both confirmed, the hours move. Your balance and history are always visible.

Everyone is good at something. Every skill counts. Nobody has to be the best.

Got time this Christmas?

Make an offer, meet new people, show your skill and make friends. Start with what you are good at, and see who needs it.

A kind community

  • New members start gently and grow trust through vouches and good references.
  • Offers from new members are checked before they go live.
  • Your private details (address, phone) stay private. You decide when to share them.
  • You can download or delete all your data in the app at any time.

Hours is for sharing skills. It is not a dating service, not counselling or therapy, and not care or nursing.
