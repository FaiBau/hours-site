Delete your Hours account

You can delete your Hours account and your data at any time. The Hours app is run by FaiBau UG (haftungsbeschränkt), Berlin.

In the app

  1. Open the Hours app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Me tab.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom, then confirm.

Your account is deleted at once. If you want a copy of your data first, tap Download my data on the same screen.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email Fai@FaiBau.de from the email address you use for Hours, with the subject "Delete my Hours account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and email address
  • Your profile: name, username, photo, bio, skills and location
  • Your private details: legal name, address, phone, insurance answer
  • Your push tokens and Inbox
  • Chat messages you sent and the text of your references and vouches
  • Open HourDeals are cancelled

What is kept

  • A nameless profile entry, the hours ledger and past HourDeals, so the other members' hour history stays correct. Nobody can see who you were.
  • Server logs are deleted automatically after a few days.
  • Where tax or commercial law requires longer storage, we keep that data only for that purpose.
